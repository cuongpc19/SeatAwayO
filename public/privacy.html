Privacy Policy

Seat Match · last updated 2026-09-08

This game is free, has no accounts, and never asks for your name, e-mail address or any other personal detail. What follows is the whole of what it stores and sends.

Saved on your own device

Your progress is kept in your browser's local storage under the key seatmatch.save.v2: the level you have reached, your stars, your gold, your boosters, and the sound and vibration switches. It never leaves your device except as described under Cloud save below, and clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

Cloud save

When the game is played on CrazyGames, that same saved progress is also handed to the CrazyGames SDK so your levels follow you between devices. It is governed by CrazyGames' own privacy policy. On any other site this does not happen.

Google Analytics

The game loads Google Analytics 4 (measurement id G-TZ865K21Z4) the first time you open a level — not when the page loads. It records that a visit happened, roughly how long it lasted, and two events of ours:

  • level_start — the level number and its difficulty
  • level_end — the level number, win or lose, how many seconds it took, and how many stars

Google derives an approximate country from your IP address. We are not given your IP address itself, and we set no advertising or cross-site identifiers. Analytics is handled under Google's privacy policy, and any browser setting or extension that blocks it will simply stop it working — the game plays exactly the same either way.

Gameplay statistics

When a level ends — and only then — one row is written to our Firebase Realtime Database so we can see which levels are too hard or too easy. The row is exactly this and nothing else:

  • the level number, its difficulty, and a fingerprint of the board's layout
  • whether it was won or lost, how many milliseconds it took, how many moves were made, and how many stars were scored
  • which boosters or revives were used, if any
  • which build of the game was played, which site it was played on, and whether Google Analytics loaded
  • the time the level ended

There is no identifier of any kind in that row — no account, no device id, no cookie, nothing that links one row to another or to you. The rows cannot be read back by anyone visiting the game; the database accepts new rows and serves none.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising sold by us, and no third-party ad network in the build.
  • No cookies of our own, and no cross-site tracking.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone beyond the two services named above.
  • The game is not aimed at children under 13 and collects nothing that could identify one.

Your choices

Clearing your browser's site data for this page erases your saved progress. Blocking googletagmanager.com stops the analytics. Because the gameplay rows carry no identifier, there is no way to single out or delete an individual player's rows — there is nothing in them to search on.
