A rule this game never had

An earlier version of this page compared all of the above against a fourth column called “leaves” — a full comb lifting off the floor — and labelled it the original. It is not. Nothing in src/engine.js removes a seat. The flag I took it from lives only in src/bench_rush.html, an abandoned August prototype that the solver script still points at.

So the column is gone, and the first column above is the real baseline: a full piece stays on the floor, stops taking anyone, and goes on being an obstacle you can shove aside.

What is actually new here

Sealing a piece when it fills kills every board tried — board 3 has seven bees and dies after one. Every board has capacity equal to its queue exactly, so every piece must fill; seal each one as it fills and the movable pieces drain away while the slots still to reach get deeper.

Honey that sets — a piece holding one bee but not yet full cannot be moved, and Smoke softens one for one drag — is the rule worth keeping. On board 19 it takes the puzzle from 89 drags to 153.

Every board is transposed: 6×10 portrait becomes 10×6 landscape. Swapping the axes relabels every coordinate, so paths, gaps and collisions map one to one and a solvable board stays exactly as solvable. It is a reflection across the diagonal rather than a rotation, which is why a board you know well does not look like itself.
