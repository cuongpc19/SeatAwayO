Privacy

This game is free, has no accounts, and never asks for your name, e-mail address or any other personal detail.

On your device. Your progress is kept in your browser under seatmatch3d.save.v1: the level you have reached, your stars, your gold, and the sound and vibration switches. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

Sent when a level ends. One row per finished level: the level number, a fingerprint of the board, its difficulty, whether you won, how long it took, how many moves, how many stars, which boosters you spent, the build number and the site it was played on. No identifier for you, and no way to join two rows into one player.

Nothing else. No advertising, no third-party analytics, no profiling. The rows are used to see which levels are too hard.
