How it plays

  1. The guest ringed in the queue goes next. Click a bench to sit them down.
  2. A coloured bench only takes guests of its colour. A grey bench takes anyone — but it locks to the first colour that sits on it.
  3. Every bench seats two guests (a wide bench, four). Fill it and the whole bench clears out.
  4. Clear the board before the clock runs out. If the next guest has nowhere legal to sit, the run ends — every board is generated with a guaranteed solution, and Hint will point at it.

Board size, colour count, seat count, grey-bench share, wide-bench share and the clock all come from the decoded pacing table — medians taken every 20 campaign levels, interpolated. The original game’s exact rule set is not recoverable from level data, so the rules above are this prototype’s own; the difficulty dials are the measured ones.
