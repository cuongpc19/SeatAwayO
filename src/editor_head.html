How it plays

    
    
      
  1. Moving seats is your only move. Press a seat anywhere on it — the seat itself or the
        passenger sitting on it — and it follows the pointer until you let go. Passengers board on their own,
        one at a time, the moment the one at the front can walk to a seat that will take them.
    2. 
      
  2. They come in through the door on the right and walk across empty floor only — seats block the way.
        A coloured seat takes only its own colour; a grey seat takes anyone.
    3. 
      
  3. A seat can only be entered over its cushion or from either side. Nobody climbs in over the backrest, so
        the tile behind a seat is no use for boarding it.
    4. 
      
  4. A seat with somebody already on it still slides, passenger and all — but a seat someone is
        currently walking to is pinned until they sit down.
    5. 
      
  5. The empty floor never grows, and in a real board it is broken into pockets. Sliding seats to join those
        pockets up is the whole puzzle. Seat everyone before the clock runs out.
    6. 
    

    
A blue tint on floor the queue can reach, a white ring on the seat they are heading for, the
      walking route and a dashed ring on a door-blocking seat are coaching overlays added here — the shipped
      game draws none of them, so they are off by default. Picking a seat up only glows that seat's own tile.
