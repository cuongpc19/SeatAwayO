Take a Seat Level Curve

Every LevelCfModel asset in the shipped APK, decoded from raw bytes and plotted. 600 campaign levels, 400 difficulty variants, 93 challenge boards — what the designers actually dialled, level by level.

Colours in play

dots = each level · line = 15-level average

The real difficulty dial. Colour count is what makes a board hard to hold in your head — it climbs fast to five, then flattens for four hundred levels. Six and seven show up only as rare spikes.

Board size vs. how much of it is used

Grid cells Occupied seats

The grid grows in hard steps — 18 cells, then 24, 28, 32, 40, 60, 77 — but the seats placed on it track it loosely. The gap between the two lines is the free space you get to manoeuvre in.

Time limit

Level 1 gets 300 s. By level 23 it is under 90 s, then it settles into a 90–165 s working band and stays there.

Grids used

Campaign levels by board shape. 5×8 carries more than half the game.

When each mechanic first appears

The teaching order, read straight off the data. Everything structural lands inside the first 45 levels; after that the game only turns existing dials. Two mechanics are held back deliberately — rotated benches until 351, three-seat benches until 355.

Method

The binary grammar

Field names came out of global-metadata.dat; element strides were brute-forced and only one candidate consumes all 1 303 assets byte-exactly.
