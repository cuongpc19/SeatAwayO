Checking a tile before you use it

  1. Seam. Duplicate the tile and butt the copies together. A visible join means regenerate — do not paper over it by blurring the edge.
  2. Saturation. Drop a #e83e48 square and a #2aced6 square on it. If either stops popping, the background is too loud.
  3. Tilt. Verticals must stay vertical. Any converging lines and it will not sit under the seats.
  4. Emptiness. The floor tile has to survive a seat on every cell. Anything eye-catching on it becomes clutter the moment the board is full.
